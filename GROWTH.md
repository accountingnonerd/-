# Growth playbook: LinkedIn

Goal set 2026-11-14: **100 new followers for every 5 posts.**

This file records what the evidence says drives reach and follows on LinkedIn, what this series
changes because of it, and how each post is measured. Research done 2026-11-14 from published
studies, most of them vendor studies quoted second-hand; dataset sizes are given so they can be
weighed. Several sources could not be opened directly, so check a figure before quoting it.

## What the evidence says

| Lever | Finding | Strength | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Format | Document (PDF carousel) posts lead engagement: about 7.0% vs 5.2% image, 4.5% text, 3.3% link posts (company pages, 1.3M posts). On personal profiles, documents reach about 1.45x the median. | Moderate on direction, weak on size | [Socialinsider 2026](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/linkedin); [AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com/blog/linkedin-algorithm) |
| Carousel size | 1080 x 1350 (4:5). Length: 7 to 12 slides in most guides; one study of 3,532 carousels found longer ones got more reactions. No data on text-heavy PDFs. | Weak, contradictory | [Flypost](https://www.flypost.io/learn/linkedin-carousel-size) |
| The fold | About 2 to 3 lines show before "see more" on a post with a document; line breaks pull the fold earlier. Keep the hook to about 110 characters. | Weak to moderate | [AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com/blog/linkedin-character-limit) |
| Links | Studies disagree. Link posts get less reach on company pages; little on personal profiles. A link in the first comment did about 2% worse than in the body in one study (287k posts). | Contested | [Ordinal](https://www.tryordinal.com/blog/linkedin-link-penalty-study); [MagicPost](https://magicpost.in/studies/linkedin-link-penalty) |
| Hashtags | 1 to 5 is best; more adds nothing. | Moderate | [Metricool](https://metricool.com/hashtags-on-linkedin/) |
| Timing | Tuesday to Thursday in every study. Hour varies: 10 am to 2 pm local is the usual window. | Moderate | [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-on-linkedin/) |
| Frequency | More posts per week were associated with more impressions per post (2M+ posts); 2 to 4 a week in another report. No study shows weekly-plus posting cannibalizing reach. | Moderate, correlational | [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-linkedin/) |
| First hour | Replying to comments was associated with about 30% more engagement. LinkedIn ranks by topic relevance and rewards meaningful comments from people who usually engage with the topic. | Moderate | [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/linkedin-algorithm/) |
| Newsletter | Publishing a newsletter's first edition invites all connections and followers by notification; every later edition notifies subscribers. | **Official** | [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/111414) |
| Follows | Follows happen on the profile. "Follow" can be set as the primary button. Comments on others' posts drive more profile visits than likes. | Weak | [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/linkedins-removing-its-creator-mode-option/706585/) |

## What changes in this series, from Agent 4

1. **The post carries a designed carousel**, 1080 x 1350, about 12 slides, at most about 30 words
   each, instead of the 14-page article as a PDF. The last slide is the follow promise.
2. **The hook names the money and the deadline** in the first two lines, under about 110
   characters.
3. **Fixed slot:** Tuesday or Wednesday, 10:00 am Eastern, scheduled through Typefully. Not
   Thanksgiving week.
4. **Every post ends with the same follow line**, naming the series and the next release.
5. **3 hashtags**, not 5.
6. **The full article goes out as a LinkedIn newsletter edition** ("One Painful Problem"), which
   only the account owner can create; the post links readers to it. The first edition invites the
   whole network.
7. **The link test:** Agent 4 puts the GitHub link in the first comment; Agent 5 puts it in the
   last line of the post. Compare impressions.
8. **Between releases, short posts** (2 to 3 a week, 24 hours apart): a lesson from the testing,
   a single screenshot tied to a deadline, a roundup carousel, a "which problem next?" post.

## What only the account owner can do

- Set **Follow** as the primary profile button (Settings, Visibility).
- Headline: for example "CPA | Free, tested tools for accountants | One Painful Problem, every
  Tuesday".
- Featured section: the newsletter, the GitHub repository, the best carousel.
- Create the newsletter and publish each article as an edition.
- For 15 minutes before and an hour after each post: reply to every comment, and write a few
  genuine comments on larger accounting and tax creators' posts. No engagement pods.

## Posting quota

The connected Typefully plan allows 10 posts a month; 3 were used by 2026-11-14 (resets 2026-12-01).

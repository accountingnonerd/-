Who gets a 1099?

Give it the list of everything a client paid this year, and it sorts the vendors into who needs a Form 1099, whose W-9 to check, and why.

  • Which file: all payments for the tax year, one line per check or charge.
  • Which rule: $2,000 for 2026 payments, $600 for 2025 and earlier.
  • Privacy: the file never leaves this computer.
  • The result is a worksheet. You must review it before anything is filed.

Who gets a 1099?

Give this page the list of everything your client paid this year. It gives back a worksheet: who needs a 1099, who needs a W-9, and the reason for every vendor.

Which file? The year's payments, one line per check or charge. In QuickBooks: Reports, then "Transaction List by Vendor", this year, Export to Excel. A check register or expense ledger works too.

No file handy?

Where your data goes: nowhere. This page reads the file on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent.

You stay in charge. The result is a worksheet to review, not a filing. It never sees a W-9, so it cannot know for sure who is a corporation.

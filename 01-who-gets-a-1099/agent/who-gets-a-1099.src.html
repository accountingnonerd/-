Who gets a 1099?

Give this page the list of everything your client paid in the tax year. It gives back a worksheet: who gets a 1099, whose W-9 to check, and the reason for every vendor.

Which file? All payments for the tax year, for example January to December 2026, one line per check or charge. In QuickBooks: Reports, then "Transaction List by Vendor", set the dates to the whole year, Export to Excel. A check register or expense ledger works too.

Which rule? $2,000 per vendor for payments made in 2026. $600 for 2025 and earlier. It will not guess at 2027.

No file handy?

Where your data goes: nowhere. This page reads the file on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent.

You stay in charge. The result is a worksheet to review, not a filing. It has not seen any W-9.

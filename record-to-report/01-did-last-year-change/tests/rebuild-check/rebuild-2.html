Did last year change?

Give me your final trial balance for last year and the client's trial balance for the same date exported today, and I will tell you whether last year's books still agree with your figures, what changed, and what it means for the return.

How to export the client's trial balance

  • QuickBooks Online: Reports, search for "Trial Balance". Set the report period to the last day of last year (for example 12/31/2025 to 12/31/2025), choose the same accounting method as your return (accrual or cash), Run report, then Export to Excel.
  • QuickBooks Desktop: Reports, Accountant & Taxes, Trial Balance. Set From and To to the last day of last year, set the basis under Customize Report if needed, then Excel, Create New Worksheet.
  • Xero: Accounting, Reports, Trial Balance. Set the date to the last day of last year, Update, then Export, Excel.

What this adds

QuickBooks and Xero can list the transactions that were changed. This compares the books with your own final figures, including the adjustments that exist only in your workpapers, and tells you what each difference means for last year's return.

Where your data goes: nowhere

The files are read and compared inside this browser window. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

You stay in charge

You stay in charge: whether to post the entry, reverse the client's change or amend last year's return is your decision.

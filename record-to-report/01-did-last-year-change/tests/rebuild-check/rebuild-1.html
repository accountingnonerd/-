Did last year change?

Give me last year's final trial balance and a trial balance for the same date exported from the client's books today, and I will tell you whether the books still agree with your final figures, what changed, what it means for last year's return, and draft a correcting entry.

How to export the trial balance

  • QuickBooks Online: Reports, Trial Balance. Set the report period to end on December 31 of last year, accrual basis (or the basis of your final), Run report, then Export to Excel.
  • QuickBooks Desktop: Reports, Accountant & Taxes, Trial Balance. Set From and To to December 31 of last year, then Excel, Create New Worksheet (or Export as CSV).
  • Xero: Accounting, Reports, Trial Balance. Set the date to 31 December of last year, Update, then Export, Excel.

What this adds

QuickBooks and Xero can list the transactions that were changed. This compares the books with your own final figures instead, including the adjustments that exist only in your workpapers, and tells you what each difference means for last year's return.

Did last year change?

Give this page two trial balances for the same date: last year's final one from your file, and one exported from the client's books today. It tells you whether last year's books still agree with what you filed, and what changed if they don't.

How to get file 2. QuickBooks Online: Reports, Trial Balance, set the date to December 31 of last year, Export to Excel. QuickBooks Desktop: Reports, Accountant & Taxes, Trial Balance, same date, Excel. Xero: Reports, Trial Balance, same date, Export to Excel.

What it adds. Your software can list transactions changed after the books were closed. This page compares the books with your final figures, including adjusting entries you made only in your workpapers, and says what each difference means for last year's return.

No files handy?

Where your data goes: nowhere. This page reads the files on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent.

You stay in charge. The result is a workpaper to review. Whether to post the entry, reverse the client's change, or amend last year's return is your decision.

# Script for testing with a real accountant

About 25 minutes, one accountant who prepares business returns from clients' books, one
observer. Watch where she stops; do not explain unless the script says to.

## Before she arrives
- A laptop with a mouse. No internet is needed.
- On the desktop: `did-last-year-change.html`, and from `tests/cases/`:
  - a folder `Harbor Light Bakery` with `02-final.xlsx` renamed `Harbor Light 2025 final TB.xlsx`
    and `02-today.xlsx` renamed `Harbor Light TB 12-31-25 exported today.xlsx`;
  - a folder `Harbor Light Bakery (adjustments)` with `05-final-workpaper.xlsx` renamed
    `TB-1 Harbor Light 2025.xlsx` and `05-today.xlsx` renamed `Harbor Light QBO TB 12-31-25.xlsx`;
  - a folder `Copper Kettle` with `09-final.xlsx` and `09-today.xlsx` (the cash-basis export).
- A calculator, a stopwatch and this sheet.

## Part 1. By hand (10 minutes, timed)
Say: *"This client's books may have been changed since you filed last year's return. Here
are last year's final trial balance and one exported today for the same date. Tell me
whether anything changed, and what you would do."* Use the first folder. Stop her at 15 minutes.
- [ ] Minutes taken: ______
- [ ] Did she find both accounts ($4,250 in Checking and Bakery Sales)? ______
- [ ] Did she say whether last year's profit changed? ______
- [ ] What would she do (amend, reverse the client's change, or post an entry)? ______

## Part 2. The page, cold (5 minutes, timed)
Say: *"A colleague sent you the file on the desktop. Try it on the second folder. Think out loud."*
- [ ] Seconds until she drops the first file: ______ (target: under 30)
- [ ] Did the right file go in each box? ______
- [ ] Seconds until she can say what is wrong: ______ (right answer: her depreciation and
      accrued wages entries were never posted; profit in the books is $11,150 higher)
- [ ] Did the rounding note worry her, or reassure her? ______
- [ ] Would she post the entry, or ask the client to post her entries in 2025? Does the page
      make clear it is one or the other? ______
- [ ] Would she print it for the file as it is? ______

## Part 3. The wrong export (3 minutes)
Say: *"Now Copper Kettle."*
- [ ] Does she understand why it will not compare, and what to export instead? ______

## Part 4. Her own files (5 minutes, optional, her choice)
If she is willing, she exports one real client's trial balance for last December 31 and
uses her own final trial balance. **She keeps the files; nothing is copied.** Note only:
- [ ] Did it read both files? If not, what layout was it (software, columns)? ______
- [ ] Did the result match what she knows about that client? ______

## Part 5. Three questions (2 minutes)
1. "Would you keep this printout in the file? What is missing?"
2. "What did you not trust?"
3. "What else at the start of a new year do you still do by hand?" (Goes into `BACKLOG.md`.)

Step 1 of 4: give the page the bank's download and the books' ledger for the same account.

Why won't the bank rec tie?

This page matches the bank's transactions to the books for one account, over one month or several. It lists what is still outstanding and how old it is, what the bank has that the books do not, the likely typos, reversed signs and duplicates, and the reconciliation that proves it.

No files yet?

What files to get

1. From the bank: on the bank's website, open the account, choose the dates (for example January 1 to March 31) and download the transactions as CSV or Excel. If the download has no balance column, keep the statement handy: you will type its beginning and ending balances.

2. From the books: the ledger detail for the same bank account, from about three months before the bank's first date to the statement date or later. Starting earlier lets the page find checks written before the bank's dates that are still outstanding.

  • QuickBooks Online: Reports, General Ledger (or Transaction Detail by Account), the dates, filter to the bank account if you like, Run report, then Export, Export to Excel.
  • QuickBooks Desktop: Reports, Accountant & Taxes, General Ledger, the dates, then Excel.
  • Xero: Reports, Account Transactions, the dates, the bank account, Update, then Export, Excel.
  • Other software: the report that lists every transaction by account, with a Date, an Amount (or Debit and Credit) and a Balance column.

Do not edit or sort either file: the page checks that each adds up.

What happens next

  1. Check what was read (you check): the dates, the balances and, if the ledger has several accounts, which one is the bank account.
  2. Decide what was found (you decide): book entries that look wrong, bank items missing from the books, and old checks.
  3. Print the reconciliation (built from your choices): the proof, the lists, the entries and the questions for the client.

Where your data goes: nowhere. This page reads the files on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent.

The page finds; you decide. It matches and explains; what to post is your call.

# Script for testing with a real accountant

About 25 minutes, one accountant who reconciles bank accounts for small businesses, one observer.
Watch where she stops; do not explain unless the script says to.

## Before she arrives
- A laptop with a mouse. No internet is needed.
- On the desktop: `why-wont-the-bank-rec-tie.html`, the two files from `sample/` (the Mapleton bank
  download and ledger), and `tests/cases/08-bank.csv` with `tests/cases/08-books-from-january.xlsx`,
  renamed `Kestrel bank Q1.csv` and `Kestrel GL from Jan.xlsx`.
- A stopwatch and this sheet.

## Part 1. By hand (10 minutes, timed)
Say: *"Here is a client's bank download for January to March and their ledger. The reconciliation
doesn't tie. Find out why."* Use the Mapleton files in Excel. Stop her at 10 minutes.
- [ ] Did she find check #1033 ($683.50 in the books, $638.50 at the bank)?
- [ ] The Birch Dental deposit entered twice? The $766.55 card batch? The stale check #998?

## Part 2. The page, cold (8 minutes, timed)
Say: *"A colleague sent you the file on the desktop. Do the same with it. Think out loud."*
- [ ] Seconds until both files are in: ______ (target: under 30)
- [ ] Does she compare the balances in step 2 and press "This is right", or scroll past? ______
- [ ] Does she understand that the two entries in 3a wait for her? Where did she hesitate? ______
- [ ] What does she choose for the stale check, and why? ______

## Part 3. A harder file (5 minutes)
Say: *"Now Kestrel. Their books file only starts in January."*
- [ ] Does she understand why the page asks her to tick two January bank lines in 3b? ______
- [ ] Does she tick them, and would she want last year's outstanding list instead? ______

## Part 4. Three questions (2 minutes)
1. "Would you keep this printout in the file? What is missing?"
2. "What did you not trust?"
3. "What else in the close would you hand to a tool like this?" (Goes into `BACKLOG.md`.)

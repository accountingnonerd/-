    Why won't the bank rec tie?

    Give it the bank's transactions and the books' ledger for the same account: it matches them, lists what is outstanding and how old it is, what the bank has that the books do not, the book entries that look wrong, and the reconciliation that proves it.

    Try it on a made-up sample.

    What files to get

    From the bank: the transactions for the months to reconcile, downloaded from the bank's website as CSV or Excel. If the download has no balance column, keep the statement handy: you will type its beginning and ending balances.

    From the books: the ledger detail for the same bank account, from about three months before the bank's first date to the statement date or later (the earlier months let the page find the checks that were still outstanding when the bank's dates begin).

    • QuickBooks Online: Reports, General Ledger. Customize: set the dates; Filter, Account: the bank account. Run report, then Export to Excel.
    • QuickBooks Desktop: Reports, Accountant & Taxes, General Ledger. Customize Report: set the dates; Filters, Account: the bank account. Then Excel, Create New Worksheet.
    • Xero: Accounting, Reports, Account Transactions. Choose the bank account and the dates, Update, then Export, Excel.
    • Other software: any general ledger detail report for the bank account with a Date column, an Amount column (or Debit and Credit) and a Balance column, exported to Excel or CSV.

    Do not edit or sort either file: the page uses the totals and balances in them to check that nothing is missing.

    What happens next

    1. Check what was read (you check): the page shows what it read from both files; you confirm it, choose the bank account if needed, and type the statement's balances if the download has none.
    2. Decide what was found (you decide): book entries that look wrong, bank items not in the books, and stale checks. The page suggests; you choose.
    3. Print the reconciliation (built from your choices): the proof, the entries to post and the questions for the client.

    Where your data goes: nowhere

    Everything runs in this browser. The files are read here and are not uploaded or sent anywhere.

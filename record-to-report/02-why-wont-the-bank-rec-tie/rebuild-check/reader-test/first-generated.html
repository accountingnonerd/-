Step 1 of 4: give the page the bank's download and the books' ledger for the same account.

Why won't the bank rec tie?

It matches the bank's lines to the books, lists what is outstanding and what the bank has that the books do not, points out the book entries that look wrong, and builds the reconciliation that proves it.

Try it on a made-up sample.

What files to get

From the bank: the transactions for the months to reconcile, downloaded from the bank's website as CSV or Excel. If the download has no balance column, keep the statement handy: you will type its beginning and ending balances.

From the books: the ledger detail for the same bank account, from about three months before the bank's first date to the statement date or later.

  • QuickBooks Online: Reports, General Ledger. Customize: set the dates, and under Filter choose the bank account. Run it, then Export, Export to Excel.
  • QuickBooks Desktop: Reports, Accountant & Taxes, General Ledger. Customize Report: set the dates; Filters, Account, the bank account. Then Excel, Create New Worksheet.
  • Xero: Accounting, Reports, Account Transactions. Choose the bank account and the dates, Update, then Export, Excel.
  • Other software: the general ledger or account detail report for the bank account, with a date, an amount (or debit and credit) and a running balance on each line, saved as Excel or CSV.

Do not edit or sort either file. The page checks that each one adds up, and an edited file will not.

What happens next

  1. Check what was read (you check): the statement dates, the balances, and which account in the books is the bank account.
  2. Decide what was found (you decide): the book entries that look wrong, how to treat what is at the bank but not in the books, and any stale checks.
  3. Print the reconciliation (built from your choices): the proof, the entries, and the questions for the client.

Where your data goes: nowhere

The files are read here, in your browser, on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent.

The page finds; you decide.

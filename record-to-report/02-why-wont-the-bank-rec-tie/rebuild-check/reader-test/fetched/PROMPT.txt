Then a list id="corrections": each "fix" (correct the entry from $A to $B, or record the voided check again: edit the transaction if its period is open, otherwise post a correcting entry), each "bank" (ask the bank to correct it), each entry entered twice (delete or void it), each "ask" (find out what it was). Or "No entries." "Questions for the client": each "ask" item, each returned item, each stale or old check, each late item. "What I read": both layouts, the number of lines and entries, the entries after the statement left out. Footer: "Made by "Why won't the bank rec tie?" version 1.0.0, on this computer. The files were read here and not sent anywhere. Arithmetic is done in whole cents. The page matches and explains; you decide what to post."

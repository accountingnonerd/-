Series 2 · Agent 1 | Why won't the bank rec tie?

Check #1033 entered as $683.50, paid by the bank as $638.50. A $4,120 customer payment entered twice. Three card sales that arrive as one $766.55 deposit. A $415 check from last August nobody cashed.

That is one quarter of one checking account. QuickBooks just says it's off.

Agent 1 of One Painful Problem, Series 2, is a free tool. Drop in the bank's download and the books' ledger (QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop or Xero), and it gives you:

→ Checks matched by number, card batches matched to the one deposit
→ The entries that look wrong: wrong amount, digits swapped, sign reversed, entered twice, paid twice
→ What the bank has that the books don't, with a suggested treatment
→ What's outstanding and how old, with stale checks flagged
→ The proof, the entries, and the questions for the client

It decides nothing for you. Until you say what each odd pair is, it won't say "it ties".

You can rebuild it yourself: one prompt pasted into an AI assistant, about 15 minutes, no coding. A sample client and the numbers you should see are included.

Before release: 28 test files, 839 checks. A skeptical-CPA review called the first version "not ready": it could tie by plugging the opening difference. That is fixed, along with everything else it found.

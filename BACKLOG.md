# Backlog

Candidate problems and reader suggestions. One gets picked per cycle. A candidate can be
killed here with a reason; that is a valid outcome.

## Candidates with evidence

### Brokerage 1099 entry sheet (strong; good for a February cycle)
One or several consolidated brokerage 1099 PDFs in, one page out with the Schedule D /
Form 8949 totals by category (short/long, covered/noncovered), wash sale adjustments,
and the 1099-DIV and 1099-INT boxes, tied to the statement's own summary.
- "Manual entry of the brokerage 1099s is very time consuming... many of my clients have
  several 1099s so a lot open/close PDFs to get to those totals." (April 2026,
  [Intuit Accountants Community](https://accountants.intuit.com/community/practice-advice-17/what-still-has-too-much-manual-follow-up-in-your-tax-workflow-today-5852))
- "a 45 page 1099-B summary statement, over a million in proceeds/basis, with 45k in wash
  sales" ([thread](https://accountants.intuit.com/community/proseries-product-discussions-9/is-there-an-easier-way-to-input-transactions-on-the-1099-b-when-there-are-wash-sales-or-other-adjustments-83114))
- "I do not want to have to answer multiple, automated underreporter letters from the IRS
  after tax season because I'm doing it wrong!" ([thread](https://accountants.intuit.com/community/proseries-tax-discussions/discussion/help-how-to-enter-stock-sales-summary-amounts-when-there-are/00/137182))
- Risk: practitioners disagree on summarizing wash sales
  ([thread](https://accountants.intuit.com/community/proseries-product-discussions-9/large-amounts-of-stock-transactions-from-1099b-with-wash-sales-81704)).
  The agent would have to follow the [Form 8949 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8949)
  exceptions exactly and say so.
- Needs: realistic synthetic composites for several brokers; scanned-PDF handling.

### PDF bank statement to a tied-out transaction list (weak evidence, crowded field)
- Evidence seen only as search snippets of AccountingWEB threads, for example
  [Convert Bank Statements in to Excel](https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/any-answers/convert-bank-statements-in-to-excel).
- Several paid converters already exist and already claim balance tie-out. Our edge
  would be thin. Leaning toward killing this unless readers ask for it.

### Year-end payroll tie-out: four Forms 941 against the W-3 (not yet researched in depth)
- Four 941 PDFs and a W-3 in, a one-page reconciliation out, line to box, with differences
  explained or flagged. January work, once per payroll client.
- Open question: payroll providers produce both sides, so how often does it actually not tie?

### Financial statement footing and cross-tie (not yet researched in depth)
- A draft set of financial statements in, a tie-out report out: every column re-added,
  every number that appears twice compared.
- Practitioner forums where this is discussed (Fishbowl, Going Concern) blocked automated
  reading, so there are no verbatim quotes yet. SEC EDGAR filings would be the test inputs.

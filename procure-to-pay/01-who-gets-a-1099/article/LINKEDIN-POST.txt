The 1099 your software won't find.

Three checks to one plumber: $700, $800, $900. Typed three different ways, and none flagged for 1099, so the software's 1099 report shows nothing. Combined, it is $2,400: over the new $2,000 limit for payments made in 2026. (The plumber is an invented test case. The way it slips through is not.)

I built a free tool that reads every payment in a client's export, flagged or not, and hands back a 1099 worksheet with a reason for every vendor. It runs on your own computer; nothing is uploaded.

It is episode 1 of a series: five accounting processes, one tool at a time, each built to work alongside the software you already have.

Then we tried to break it, and our own tests fooled us for a while. That story is in the article.

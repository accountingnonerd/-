What's really collectible?

Give this page the client's A/R aging detail. It gives back a receivables review: what to clean up in the ledger, who to chase first and with what words, and the allowance and write-offs to support at the close.

Which report? QuickBooks Online: Reports, A/R Aging Detail, the month-end date, Export to Excel. QuickBooks Desktop: Reports, Customers & Receivables, A/R Aging Detail, Excel. Xero: Reports, Aged Receivables Detail, Export to Excel. The detail, not the summary: the page needs each invoice and each payment.

What it adds. The aging lists what is open. This page reads every line: it matches payments and credits that were never applied, spots invoices entered twice, ranks customers by money and age, drafts the reminders, and supports the allowance.

No file handy?

Where your data goes: nowhere. This page reads the file on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent.

You propose; the client decides. Applying credits, writing off and sending letters are the client's decisions; the allowance rates are your judgment.

What's really collectible?

Drop a client's A/R aging detail and get back a receivables review: what to clean up in the ledger first, who to chase with a draft letter each, and the allowance and write-offs at the close.


Which report to export

  • QuickBooks Online: Reports, A/R Aging Detail (not the summary), Export to Excel.
  • QuickBooks Desktop: Reports, Customers & Receivables, A/R Aging Detail, Excel, Create new worksheet.
  • Xero: Reports, Aged Receivables Detail (not the summary), Export, Excel.

What it adds: it applies credits and payments sitting unapplied, finds customers in credit and payments that may be posted to the wrong customer, spots invoices entered twice, re-ages by due date, ranks who to chase with a draft letter each, and lays out the allowance and write-offs.

Where your data goes: nowhere. The file is read in this browser and never uploaded.

# Script for testing with a real accountant

About 25 minutes, one accountant who does monthly closes for small businesses, one observer. Watch
where she stops; do not explain unless the script says to.

## Before she arrives
- A laptop with a mouse. No internet is needed.
- On the desktop: `whats-really-collectible.html`, and from `tests/cases/`:
  - `03-planted-unapplied-credits.xlsx` renamed `Granite AR aging 10-31-26.xlsx`;
  - `17-planted-netting-and-misposting.xlsx` renamed `Falcon AR aging 10-31-26.xlsx`;
  - `08-old-and-small-balances.xlsx` renamed `Copperline AR aging 10-31-26.xlsx`, with a sticky note:
    "Allowance on the books: $15,000. Loss rates: 1, 2, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100%".
- A calculator, a stopwatch and this sheet.

## Part 1. By hand (10 minutes, timed)
Say: *"Here is the October aging for a client. Tell me what you would clean up before anyone calls a
customer, and who you would call first."* Use Granite. Stop her at 15 minutes.
- [ ] Minutes taken: ______
- [ ] Did she find the four payments that match invoices exactly? The customer who paid twice? ______

## Part 2. The page, cold (5 minutes, timed)
Say: *"A colleague sent you the file on the desktop. Try it on Falcon. Think out loud."*
- [ ] Seconds until she drops the file: ______ (target: under 30)
- [ ] Does she understand the payment posted to the wrong customer? ______
- [ ] Would she send the first three letters as drafted? What would she change? ______

## Part 3. The close (5 minutes)
Say: *"Now Copperline, at the close."*
- [ ] Does she type the rates and the allowance on the books? Does the entry match what she would post? ______
- [ ] Does she keep or write off the invoices over a year old? Why? ______

## Part 4. Three questions (5 minutes)
1. "Would you keep this printout in the file? What is missing?"
2. "What did you not trust?"
3. "What else in the monthly close would you hand to a tool like this?" (Goes into `BACKLOG.md`.)

# Script for testing with a real accountant

About 25 minutes, one accountant who prepares small-business returns, one observer. Watch where
she stops; do not explain unless the script says to.

## Before she arrives
- A laptop with a mouse. No internet is needed.
- On the desktop: `does-the-1099k-tie.html`, and from `tests/cases/`:
  - `01-square-juniper-street-2025.csv` renamed `Juniper Street Square 2025.csv`;
  - `08-square-one-location.csv` renamed `Copper Mill Square 2025.csv`, with a sticky note:
    "1099-K box 1a: $41,342.05";
  - `04-paypal-ridgeline-prints-2025.csv` renamed `Ridgeline PayPal 2025.csv`.
- A calculator, a stopwatch and this sheet.

## Part 1. By hand (10 minutes, timed)
Say: *"This café's 1099-K came in. Here is their Square export for the year. What goes on line 1,
and does it tie to the deposits?"* Use Juniper Street. Stop her at 15 minutes.
- [ ] Minutes taken: ______
- [ ] Did she separate sales tax, tips and cash? ______
- [ ] Did she notice gift card redemptions? ______

## Part 2. The page, cold (5 minutes, timed)
Say: *"A colleague sent you the file on the desktop. Try it on Copper Mill. Think out loud."*
- [ ] Seconds until she types box 1a or drops the file: ______ (target: under 30)
- [ ] Does she understand the $2,184.00 difference and what to ask the client? ______
- [ ] Does she understand why the return figures say "not for the return yet"? ______

## Part 3. PayPal (5 minutes)
Say: *"Now Ridgeline."*
- [ ] What does she make of the no-fee transfers from Dana Rivera? ______
- [ ] Would she post the entry? Does "Owner contributions" make sense to her? ______

## Part 4. Her judgment (3 minutes)
- [ ] Does she tick "leave out sales tax" or "leave out tips"? Why? ______
- [ ] Is the default (include them, as the 1099-K does) what she would do? ______

## Part 5. Three questions (2 minutes)
1. "Would you keep this printout in the tax file? What is missing?"
2. "What did you not trust?"
3. "What else from tax season would you hand to a tool like this?" (Goes into `BACKLOG.md`.)

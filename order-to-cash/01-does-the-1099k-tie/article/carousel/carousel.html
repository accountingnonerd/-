The rule

Gross means gross.

"…without regard to any adjustments for credits, cash equivalents, discount amounts, fees, refunded amounts, shipping amounts, or any other amounts."IRS Instructions for Form 1099-K, box 1a
"…the client may receive an IRS notice if the gross receipts don't reflect amounts from the 1099-K."Intuit ProConnect help

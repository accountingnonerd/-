Does the 1099-K tie?

Drop in a client's year of Square, Stripe or PayPal transactions and get back what the Form 1099-K should say, the figures for the return, the tie to the bank deposits, and a year-end entry.

See it work on a made-up sample

Which export to get

Square: Transactions, Export, Transactions CSV.

Stripe: Reports, Balance, itemized balance change from activity.

PayPal: Activity, download, CSV.

Why it matters

The 1099-K reports gross: every card and app payment before refunds and fees. The bank sees payouts, after them. Gross receipts reported below the 1099-K may bring an IRS notice.

Where your data goes: nowhere

The file is read in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

You stay in charge

Every figure is shown with how it was reached. Your judgment decides what goes on the return.

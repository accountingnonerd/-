Does the 1099-K tie?

Give this page a client's year of card or app payments from Square, Stripe or PayPal. It gives back what the 1099-K should say, the figures for the return, how they tie to the bank deposits, and the entry for books that recorded deposits as sales.

Which file? Square: Transactions, set the dates to the whole year, Export, Transactions CSV. Stripe: Reports, Balance, the whole year, Download the itemized "balance change from activity". PayPal: Activity, Statements, Activity download, all transactions, CSV.

Why it matters. The 1099-K reports gross: before refunds and fees, with sales tax and tips inside. The bank sees only the deposits. If gross receipts on the return come in below the 1099-K, the IRS may send a notice.

No file handy?

Where your data goes: nowhere. This page reads the file on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent.

You stay in charge. Whether sales tax and tips belong in gross receipts, and whether a transfer was personal, are your calls. The page shows the amounts and says which rule applies.

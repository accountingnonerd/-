Series 1 · Agent 5 | The year-end fixed asset review, done from the general ledger

A $6,900 door in Repairs. A $5,600 sprayer in Small Tools. $16,000 from a sold truck credited straight to Vehicles.

Last year's depreciation schedule knew about none of them. All three were sitting in the client's general ledger.

Agent 5 of One Painful Problem is a free tool that reads the ledger from QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop or Xero and gives you:

→ Purchases recorded as assets, with trade-ins, land and loan payments flagged
→ Every bill over $2,500 in repairs, supplies and tools, left for you to decide: capitalize or expense
→ Sales and write-offs, with the gain or loss worked out
→ A check against last year's schedule and this year's depreciation
→ A workpaper you can file: journal entries, the balances after them, and the questions for the client

It decides nothing for you. It does the reading.

New this time: you can rebuild it yourself. One prompt pasted into an AI assistant, about 15 minutes, no coding. A sample client and the numbers you should see are included, so you can check that your copy works.

Before release: 22 test files, 760 checks. A skeptical-CPA review found 9 wrong answers in the first version. All are fixed.

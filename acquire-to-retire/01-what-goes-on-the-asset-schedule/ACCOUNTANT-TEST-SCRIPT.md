# Script for testing with a real accountant

About 25 minutes, one accountant who prepares year-end returns for small businesses, one observer.
Watch where she stops; do not explain unless the script says to.

## Before she arrives
- A laptop with a mouse. No internet is needed.
- On the desktop: `what-goes-on-the-asset-schedule.html`, and the two files from `sample/`
  (the Ridgeline ledger and last year's schedule), plus `tests/cases/20-trade-in-payments-land.xlsx`
  renamed `Prairie Grain GL 2026.xlsx`.
- A sticky note: "Tax software depreciation for 2026: $24,610.00".
- A stopwatch and this sheet.

## Part 1. By hand (10 minutes, timed)
Say: *"Here is a client's 2026 general ledger and last year's depreciation schedule. Tell me what
goes on this year's schedule and what entries you would propose."* Use the Ridgeline ledger in
Excel. Stop her at 10 minutes.
- [ ] Did she find the sprayer and the door in expense accounts? The truck sale credited to Vehicles?
- [ ] What did she decide about the laptops?

## Part 2. The page, cold (8 minutes, timed)
Say: *"A colleague sent you the file on the desktop. Do the same with it. Think out loud."*
- [ ] Seconds until she drops the file: ______ (target: under 30)
- [ ] Does she press "The accounts are right" in step 2, or scroll past it? ______
- [ ] Does she understand she must decide each bill? Where did she hesitate? ______
- [ ] Does she find the truck's cost and depreciation on the schedule and type them? ______

## Part 3. A harder file (5 minutes)
Say: *"Now Prairie Grain."*
- [ ] Does she agree that the six $912.40 payments are loan payments, and with the trade-in and land notes? ______

## Part 4. Three questions (2 minutes)
1. "Would you keep this printout in the file? What is missing?"
2. "What did you not trust?"
3. "What else at year end would you hand to a tool like this?" (Goes into `BACKLOG.md`.)

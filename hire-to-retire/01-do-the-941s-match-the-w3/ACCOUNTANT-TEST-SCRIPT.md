# Script for testing with a real accountant

About 25 minutes, one accountant who does payroll year-end, one observer. Watch where she
stops; do not explain unless the script says to.

## Before she arrives
- A laptop with a mouse. No internet is needed.
- On the desktop: `do-the-941s-match-the-w3.html`, and from `tests/cases/`:
  - `02-year-end-packet.pdf` renamed `Calle Ocho Kitchen 2026 payroll packet.pdf`;
  - a folder `Harbor Freight 2026 payroll` with the five `04-...` files;
  - `09-q2-941-scanned.pdf` renamed `Brightwater Q2 941.pdf`.
- A printout of the Calle Ocho packet, a calculator, a stopwatch and this sheet.

## Part 1. By hand (10 minutes, timed)
Say: *"Here is a client's 2026 payroll packet. Please check that the 941s agree with the W-3,
the way you would in January. Tell me when you would sign off."* Stop her at 15 minutes.
- [ ] Minutes taken: ______
- [ ] Did she check box 1? Boxes 2, 3, 5, 7? The taxes against boxes 4 and 6? ______
- [ ] Did she allow for the Additional Medicare Tax or line 7? ______

## Part 2. The page, cold (5 minutes, timed)
Say: *"A colleague sent you the file on the desktop. Try it on Calle Ocho. Think out loud."*
- [ ] Seconds until she gives it the file: ______ (target: under 30)
- [ ] Seconds until she can say whether it agrees: ______
- [ ] Anything she read aloud as a question: ______
- [ ] Does she understand the tax check in her own words? ______
- [ ] Would she print it for the file as it is? ______

## Part 3. The provider switch (5 minutes)
Say: *"Now Harbor Freight. All five files are in the folder."*
- [ ] Did she give it all five at once? ______
- [ ] In her words, what is wrong? ______ (Right answer: the new provider's W-3 leaves out
      the first half of the year; every difference equals Q1 + Q2.)
- [ ] What would she do? ______ (Right answer: get the old provider's W-2s filed, or a
      corrected W-3 and W-2s from the new provider, before the deadline.)

## Part 4. The scan (2 minutes)
Say: *"This one came from a client by email."* Give it the scanned Q2.
- [ ] Does she understand why it cannot be read, and what to ask for? ______

## Part 5. Three questions (3 minutes)
1. "Would you keep this printout in the payroll file? What is missing?"
2. "What did you not trust?"
3. "What else in payroll year-end do you still do by hand?" (Goes into `BACKLOG.md`.)

Do the 941s match the W-3?

Give this page the client's four quarterly Forms 941 and the W-3 for the year, as PDFs. It reads them and gives back the reconciliation the IRS tells employers to keep, box by box.

Reading the forms...

What it adds. Your payroll provider files both forms, but proving they agree is left to you. When the client changed providers, amended a quarter or ran some payroll by hand, they often don't agree. The IRS matches them about two years later and sends a letter.

Which files? The filed copies from the payroll provider, or the IRS forms you filled in. A provider's year-end packet with everything in one PDF works too. Scans cannot be read.

Which rule? The IRS General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3 (2026): W-3 boxes 2, 3, 5 and 7 must agree with the year's 941s, and the 941 taxes should be about twice W-3 boxes 4 and 6.

Where your data goes: nowhere. This page reads the files on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent.

You stay in charge. The result is a reconciliation to review and sign. It does not file or correct anything.

Do the 941s Match the W-3?, version 1.0.0. One Painful Problem series, Hire-to-Retire, episode 1. The tests it passed and the prompt that rebuilds it are published with it.

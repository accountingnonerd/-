Do the 941s match the W-3?

Drop in a client's filed Forms 941 for the year and the Form W-3, and get back the year-end reconciliation of the W-3 against the four quarters, ready to print and keep.

Reading the forms...
  • What it adds. The payroll provider files both forms, but proving they agree is left to you. The IRS matches them about two years later and sends a letter.
  • Which files. Filed copies from the provider, filled-in IRS forms, or one year-end packet. Scans cannot be read.
  • Which rule. The IRS General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3 (2026): boxes 2, 3, 5 and 7 against the 941s, and the tax check (employer plus employee).
  • Changed provider? Drop in both W-3s.
  • Private. Files never leave this computer. The result is a reconciliation you must review and sign.
